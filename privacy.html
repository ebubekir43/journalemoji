Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

JournalEmoji is designed with privacy in mind. Your journal entries, moods, notes, photos, and other personal journal content are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly use a feature that exports or shares that content.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how JournalEmoji ("JournalEmoji", "the App"), developed and operated by kygsxz (Ebubekir Kaygısız) ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the JournalEmoji mobile application.

By using JournalEmoji, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

2. Journal and Mood Data

JournalEmoji allows you to create personal journal content, including mood records, text entries, notes, photos, and related information.

This personal journal content is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores the contents of your private journal entries as part of the App's normal operation.

JournalEmoji does not use the contents of your private journal entries for advertising purposes.

Exporting and Sharing

If you choose to export, back up, or share content using features provided by JournalEmoji, the information you select may leave the App according to the action you choose. For example, content you intentionally share may be provided to another application or service selected by you.

You are responsible for deciding where exported or shared copies of your journal information are stored or sent.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The free version of JournalEmoji may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

When advertisements are requested, Google's advertising services may process information such as IP address, device or account identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and other technical information for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, advertising personalization.

JournalEmoji does not send the text of your journal entries, your mood records, your private notes, or your journal photos to Google Mobile Ads for advertising.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

More information about Google's privacy practices is available at: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Advertising Privacy Choices

Where required or available, JournalEmoji may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising consent and privacy choices.

The advertising options available to you may vary depending on your location, applicable law, Google services, and your previous consent choices.

Where the App provides an advertising privacy settings option, you may use it to review available privacy choices.

5. Firebase Services

JournalEmoji may use Google Firebase services that are necessary for technical application functionality.

The current version may use Firebase Authentication for technical, anonymous authentication. Anonymous authentication does not require you to provide JournalEmoji with a name, email address, or password.

The App does not use Firebase Authentication to upload the contents of your private journal to our servers.

JournalEmoji does not currently use Google Analytics for Firebase or Firebase Crashlytics for collecting analytics or crash reports. If our use of Firebase materially changes in a way that affects your privacy, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Firebase services are provided by Google and may process technical information necessary to provide their services in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

JournalEmoji offers optional purchases, which may include Premium subscriptions, ad-free subscriptions, and one-time lifetime purchases.

Purchases on the Android version of JournalEmoji are processed by Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your full payment card or bank account details.

The App may receive and process purchase-related information from Google Play, such as product identifiers, purchase status, purchase tokens, and entitlement information, when necessary to determine which features you are entitled to use and to restore purchases.

Payment processing and related information are also subject to Google Play's terms and privacy policies.

7. Device Permissions

JournalEmoji may request device permissions when they are necessary for features you choose to use. Depending on your device and App version, these may include access required for notifications, photos or media, or biometric authentication.

Permissions are used only for the functionality associated with them. You can manage device permissions through your Android system settings.

8. Notifications and Reminders

If you enable reminders, JournalEmoji may schedule notifications on your device. Reminder settings and notification functionality are used to provide the reminders you configure.

You can disable notifications through JournalEmoji or your device settings, depending on the available options.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your private journal entries or personal journal content.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide specific App functionality, including Google services used for advertising, Firebase functionality, and Google Play Billing.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request, or where reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, security, and users.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Journal content stored locally remains on your device until you delete that content, reset applicable App data, or remove it through other available device or App controls.

Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored App data, subject to Android backup, restore, device, and operating-system behavior. For this reason, you should not rely solely on uninstalling the App as a guaranteed method of deleting every possible copy or backup.

Copies that you have intentionally exported, shared, or backed up outside JournalEmoji must be managed or deleted from the destination where you stored them.

Information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to their own policies and legal obligations.
